PA Governance Radar

A dashboard visualizing Medicare Advantage prior-authorization denial-rate variance across 61 contracts. The per-contract breakdown is a synthetic reconstruction calibrated to match real, cited aggregate statistics publicly disclosed under CMS-0057-F — it is not Centene's actual per-contract data.

About this data

Under CMS-0057-F, U.S. health payers were required to publicly post CY2025 prior-authorization metrics by March 31, 2026. This page distinguishes exactly two kinds of numbers: aggregate figures that are real and cited from public reporting, and per-contract figures that are synthetically generated to match those aggregates. Nothing on this page comes from Centene's internal claims or utilization-management systems.

Real & cited Aggregate statistics

  • 61 reporting Medicare Advantage contracts
  • Denial rates ranging from 1.9% to 24.6% across contracts
  • 94.6% appeal-overturn rate
  • CMS-0057-F public disclosure deadline: March 31, 2026

As reported by Healthcare Dive and aggregated by AuthDenied (2026), based on Centene Corporation's CY2025 CMS-0057-F disclosures.

Synthetic Per-contract dataset

The 61 individual contract rows below — their IDs, states, plan types, exact denial rates, and appeal volumes — are generated by a seeded random-number generator (seed 2026), calibrated only so the dataset's overall min, max, and aggregate overturn rate match the real cited figures at left. The real per-contract breakdown is scattered across dozens of separate payer-hosted disclosure pages and was not scraped or reproduced here.

Every synthetic figure on this page is marked with this orange badge or the "SYNTHETIC" table caption below.
